# Engineering Maths & Business Portfolio

Welcome to my commercial and analytical engineering portfolio. As a Mechatronics Engineering student, I believe that a great engineer doesn't just design things that work; they design things that are commercially viable, financially sound, and of the highest quality.

This repository demonstrates my "dual-competency" by combining applied engineering mathematics with high-level commercial, quality, and business management principles.

---

### 📊 Applied Engineering Business & Commercial Principles

These projects demonstrate my understanding of the financial and commercial realities of manufacturing and automation.

*   **[Activity-Based Costing (ABC) Analysis](./05-activity-based-costing)**
    *   **What it is:** A deep-dive into true production costing for a complex mechatronic product (The RoboPod).
    *   **Key Findings:** Identified CNC machining and electronics integration as major cost drivers. Modelled the true cost per unit (£10,650) and proposed Lean manufacturing solutions to improve profitability.
    *   **Skills:** Activity-Based Costing, Cost Driver Analysis, Profitability Modelling.

*   **[Quality & Value Management](./06-quality-and-value-management)**
    *   **What it is:** A technical evaluation of ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 within an industrial automation setting. 
    *   **Key Findings:** Conducted a Value Analysis exercise using decision matrices and FAST mapping. Identified a £900 per unit saving by redesigning a manual wiring harness into a plug-and-play modular system.
    *   **Skills:** ISO Standards, Total Quality Management (TQM), PDCA, Value Engineering.

*   **[Business & Trade Case Study](./07-business-and-trade-case-study)**
    *   **What it is:** An analysis of Eurocraft Technologies, exploring how internal engineering functions (R&D, fabrication) combine with external trade considerations.
    *   **Key Findings:** Evaluated strategic sourcing, supply chain management, IP protection, and how these create a competitive advantage in the UK manufacturing sector.
    *   **Skills:** Supply Chain Management, Strategic Analysis, Risk Management.

*   **[Prototype Cost Analysis](./03-prototype-cost-analysis)**
    *   **What it is:** A financial model calculating the Bill of Materials (BOM), labour, and contingency costs for a mechatronic prototype.
    *   **Skills:** Financial Modelling, Cost Analysis, Google Sheets.

### 🧮 Numerical Methods & Applied Mathematics

These projects demonstrate my ability to use code and applied mathematics to solve real-world engineering problems.

*   **[Python Numerical Methods Solver](./02-python-numerical-methods)**
    *   **What it is:** A Python script using the Newton-Raphson method to find the roots of complex polynomial equations.
    *   **Key Learning:** Translating mathematical theories into functional Python code using NumPy and Matplotlib. Directly applicable to kinematic paths and dynamic system responses.
    *   **Files:** Includes the report, the `.py` script, and the interactive Colab notebook (`.ipynb`).

*   **[Battery Life & Power Budget Calculator](./01-battery-life-calculator)**
    *   **What it is:** A practical tool built in Google Sheets to estimate the battery life of an IoT sensor node based on current draw.
    *   **Key Learning:** Applying Ohm's Law and power budgeting to real-world IoT hardware.
    *   **Skills:** Applied Mathematics, Power Calculations, Google Sheets.
